# Clinical body atlas assets

These WebP renders are neutral clinical body-map references generated from
MakeHuman Community 1.3 parametric meshes.

- Source project: https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman
- Generated-model license: CC0 1.0
- Profiles: infant, early child, child, adolescent, late adolescent, adult,
  and older adult
- Variants: female and male; front, back, left, and right
- Rendering: neutral material, no patient image, no diagnostic annotation
- Asset dimensions: 560 x 920 WebP
- Product coordinate surface: 240 x 400

The scalp view remains a purpose-built orientation diagram because a full-body
top render introduces shoulders into the selectable scalp surface.

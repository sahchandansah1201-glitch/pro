# Clinical body atlas assets

These WebP renders are original flat clinical body-map references generated
from MakeHuman Community 1.3 parametric meshes.

- Source project: https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman
- Generated-model license: CC0 1.0
- Profiles: infant, early child, child, adolescent, late adolescent, adult,
  and older adult
- Variants: female and male; front, back, left, and right
- Rendering: light figure, dark medical contour and restrained surface detail;
  no patient image and no diagnostic annotation
- Asset dimensions: 720 x 1200 WebP
- Product coordinate surface: 240 x 400

The generation script is `scripts/render-clinical-body-line-atlas.py`. A
third-party screenshot informed the visual direction only; no third-party
image, tracing, or interface asset is included in these files.

The scalp view remains a purpose-built orientation diagram because a full-body
top render introduces shoulders into the selectable scalp surface.
